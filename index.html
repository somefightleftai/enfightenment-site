Become Enfightened.

Enfighten is wisdom earned through the fight. Forty years of martial arts — karate, jiu-jitsu — forty years of yoga, merged into one idea: the fight is what keeps you sharp, calm, and still going.

The mark is a peace sign with teeth. The peace is what you’re after. The teeth are what it costs. You don’t get to skip the rough patch — you go through it, and you come out the other side, higher. Then it starts again, on a different level.

I’m 62 and I still train every day. Karate, jiu-jitsu, yoga, BMX. Not fighting against life — that’s how you earn another day. That’s the whole thing.
