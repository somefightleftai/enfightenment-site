01 — My Story

40 years on the mat.

I started training over 40 years ago. I'm 62 now, and I'm still on the mat.

Karate · Yoga · Jiu-Jitsu · Muay Thai · MMA

Every round taught me something about fear, and about letting go of my beliefs. ENFIGHTEN is what the fight has taught me over the years.

I had many inspirations: people still training late in life. Seeing them made it real. Others made it possible for me. Hopefully I can make it possible for you.
