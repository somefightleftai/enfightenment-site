The Brand

THE ENLIGHTENED FIGHT

ENFIGHTEN is enlightenment through the fight. Not the fight against someone — the opponent is always yourself. It’s the fight to grow: to build the body, sharpen the mind, and evolve the spirit. Health, discipline, and human development, forged through the daily fight to become better.

Founder — Michael Weiner.

62 years of life, 40 on the mat — karate, yoga, jiu-jitsu, now Muay Thai and MMA. I've been through the ups and downs — family, finances, health, all of it. And what I've learned is this: the only way out is through. You have to walk through the fire to find your way to freedom, to live your best life. There are different paths through it — and the enlightened path, the enlightened fight, is the one that leads, I believe, to your greatest wellbeing and happiness. This is the path I've chosen. I think you will too. ENFIGHTEN is the gear for the fight in life — the one we all have to walk on our own. There's no one way. We're all individuals — but we're all in this together. So keep going. You'll find your own way through. And we're here to help, to guide, to inspire — and to provide some gear.
