The Idea

Cool under pressure
That's the whole idea

Most people think they've got a cool mind — until the pressure hits, and it's gone. Calm without the fight underneath it isn't calm. It's just untested. The fight is the ground floor. Build the body, build the strength, build the spirit — and that's what earns you the cool mind, the kind that holds when it's tested. Like yoga: you learn the postures and make the body strong before you can sit and still the mind. Strong spirit first. Cool mind because of it. They work together — but only if you did the work underneath.

First

Strong Spirit

The fight. The ground floor. Build the body, build the discipline. The asana before the stillness. This is what the calm stands on.

Then

Cool Mind

Calm that holds when it's tested. Act without being swept up. See clearly, move clean — because you built the fight underneath it.

Coaching

Become Enfightened

“It takes a long time to become young.” — Pablo Picasso

Forty years in — Shotokan karate, yoga from Iyengar to Ashtanga, jiu-jitsu the Danaher way. But it was never just the training. Underneath it: Zen, Buddhism, the sutras — the whole unseen part, all the instruction I was given, all the good food I had to digest for myself. And a life that made me use it and develop it. Parents, kids, money, trauma — learning to walk through the fire, because the only way out is through. You don't learn this from books. You live it. It's the only way.

You do the work daily — the grunt work, the body work, the discipline. Not to arrive somewhere, because the journey's the reward — the moment you think you've got it, you don't. It's a spiral. You meet the same thing again and again, and each time around you level up in how you handle it. You can't force the moment it opens. It just happens when you let go and the universe aligns. You don't know exactly when — so you just keep training. Not hoping. You just do the work. Chop wood, carry water.

And it all relates to longevity. Longevity isn't just diet and exercise. A huge part of it is how you think — how that thinking wears on the body, or helps it: helps it grow, helps it thrive, helps it glow. That's the hardest part as you age, because you've lived, accumulated, gotten set in your ways. You have to let it go so you can let it glow — get your youthful exuberance back, the movement, the joy.

There's no single path. Everyone walks their own — they're all different, and yet somehow all the same. Many roads up the mountain, but they lead to the same place. I can help guide you on your journey — help you get stronger, fitter, healthier, more youthful. But I can't do it for you. You have to be ready.
